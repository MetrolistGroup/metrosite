### Listen History

Take control of what gets remembered:

- **Pause listen history**: stop tracking your listening activity on the spot.
- **Clear listen history**: wipe everything clean with one action.

The same two options are available for **search history**. Full control, no questions asked.

### Screenshot Prevention

Flip **Disable screenshots** on and the app view is blocked from screenshots and hidden in the recent apps switcher. What you listen to is your business.
