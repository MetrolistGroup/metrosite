An account is optional: you can browse the app without one. However, some songs may not play without signing in.

1. Open Metrolist: you'll be dropped straight on the homescreen.
2. Tap the **profile icon** in the top right corner.
3. Tap **Login**.
4. Sign in with your Google account when prompted.
5. Once complete, the app will restart automatically.
6. Your YouTube Music playlists will appear in the app.

You can also log in with a **token** instead. Tap **Login with Token** and paste the following:

```
***INNERTUBE COOKIE*** =
***VISITOR DATA*** =
***DATASYNC ID*** =
***ACCOUNT NAME*** =
***ACCOUNT EMAIL*** =
***ACCOUNT CHANNEL HANDLE*** =
```

> **Note**: Recommendations are computed locally: they may not match what you see on YouTube Music.

### Switching Accounts

1. Tap the **profile icon** in the top right.
2. Select **Sign Out**.
3. Follow the login steps above with a different account.

### Troubleshooting Login

If the login flow doesn't complete:

- Make sure you have an active internet connection.
- Close and reopen Metrolist, then try again.
- Clear your browser's cache if the login page doesn't load.
- If issues persist, check the [Troubleshooting](/en/docs/troubleshooting) guides.
